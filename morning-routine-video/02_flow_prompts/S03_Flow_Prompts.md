## IF A SHOT COMES OUT WRONG
- **The fire looks too realistic or scary:** add *"cute, cartoon, family-friendly"* after the file name.
- **The coffee cup turns into a jerry can or petrol can:** add *"the character pouring is a white ceramic coffee cup, not a fuel can"*.
- **Blood cells look gory:** add *"stylised, clean, glossy, not gory"*.
