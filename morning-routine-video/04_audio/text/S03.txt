Mistake number two: drinking coffee before you drink water.
Think about what happens overnight. You've gone seven, eight, or nine hours without a single drop of water, constantly losing moisture through breath and perspiration. You wake up in a naturally dehydrated state, which means your blood volume is slightly lower and more concentrated.
So what do most people reach for first? Caffeine. When you pour a heavy stimulant into a dehydrated, already cortisol-elevated system, it's like throwing gasoline onto a flickering fire. It drives your stress hormones even higher, leaving you jittery by 9:30 AM and crashing by mid-morning.
Here's a key metabolic insight: much of what feels like intense hunger at 10:00 or 11:00 AM often isn't a need for calories at all—it can be mild dehydration wearing a mask. Rehydrating first with water and essential minerals completely changes how your body handles energy and appetite for the rest of the morning.
