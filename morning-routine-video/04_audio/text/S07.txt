The seventh mistake is the overarching master villain: starting your day in full survival mode.
When you combine phone notifications, instant caffeine on an empty stomach, rushing around in the dark, and stress before 8:00 AM, your brain receives one clear message: danger.
Your adrenal glands flood your system with chronic cortisol. And from an evolutionary standpoint, when your body believes it's under threat, its priority is survival—not fat loss. It actively holds onto visceral fat around your heart, liver, and kidneys as a protective energy store.
When people struggle with stubborn fat despite dieting, it's rarely a lack of willpower. It's often that their morning routine is signaling constant survival stress to their metabolism.
